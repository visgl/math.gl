# NASA overview imagery

These 2048 × 1024 geographic JPEGs provide complete day/night coverage before
remote detail tiles arrive, including the poles. Together they are about 386 KiB.
They are unmodified NASA GIBS WMS responses downloaded on 2026-10-26.

- `day.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble, `BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry`.
- `night.jpg`: NASA Black Marble, `VIIRS_Black_Marble`, `TIME=2016-01-01`.

Source endpoint: `https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi`.
Both requests use `SERVICE=WMS`, `REQUEST=GetMap`, `VERSION=1.1.1`, `STYLES=`,
`FORMAT=image/jpeg`, `SRS=EPSG:4326`, `BBOX=-180,-90,180,90`, `WIDTH=2048`,
and `HEIGHT=1024`, plus the layer/time above.

Imagery credit: NASA Earth Observatory and NASA GIBS/ESDIS.
See [Blue Marble](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble/)
and [Earth at Night](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/).
These images are observed Earth imagery, not generated illustrations. Night lights
are a fixed historical composite; the date control changes only solar illumination.

## Cartographic data

`us-states-10m.json` is the US Atlas 3.0.1 state TopoJSON, derived from the US Census Bureau at 1:10 million scale. Downloaded from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/us-atlas@3.0.1/states-10m.json. See `us-atlas-LICENSE.txt` (ISC). The mask includes the 50 states and District of Columbia.

`h3-face-centers.json` contains the 20 face centers from H3 v4.3.0 `src/h3lib/lib/faceijk.c`: https://github.com/uber/h3/blob/v4.3.0/src/h3lib/lib/faceijk.c. See `h3-LICENSE.txt` and `h3-NOTICE.txt` (Apache-2.0). The cell grid is generated by h3-js.
