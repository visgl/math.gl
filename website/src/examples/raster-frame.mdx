# Raster coordinate frames

A raster's array frame and CRS frame are separate. This recipe composes math.gl `Matrix3`
transforms and `SpatialReference` metadata to place samples explicitly, without treating a CRS
identifier, storage chunk, or OME scale as a complete georeference.

Select north-up, point-sampled, rotated/skewed or stored-axis-permuted fixtures. White points show
sample locations, blue outlines their sample envelope, and orange outlines declared cell coverage.
Area registration maps sample index `i` to grid coordinate `i + 0.5`; point registration maps it to
`i`. Point nodes alone do not establish cell coverage. Hover samples to inspect inverse errors.

The six affine coefficients map columns/rows into stored CRS components. An explicit permutation
then maps stored components into display XY. Units remain declared metadata: the recipe does not
convert feet to meters, infer axis conventions from an EPSG name, or reproject coordinates.
Use a separately prepared projection-engine agnostic converter when changing CRS. Display scaling
is uniform and subtracts the fixture origin; the underlying source coordinates retain their units.

This is an example-owned composition, with no new public API or projection-engine dependency.
CF, OME-NGFF, GeoTIFF and GDAL metadata interpretation remains in loaders.gl. In particular, OME
scale/translation can describe an array frame distinct from the CRS georeference, and every
multiscale level needs its own authoritative transform. Rotated/skewed grids retain their affine
frame; do not approximate them by an axis-aligned TileMatrix.
