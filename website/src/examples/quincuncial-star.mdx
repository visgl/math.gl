Compare Peirce quincuncial with Berghaus star. Change the central meridian to see
coastlines and the orange polygon split at new cuts; hover to inspect coordinates.

Both use the same `MapEngine` point, line, polygon, viewport fitting and picking
pipeline. Neither is a hinged polyhedron. The Peirce elliptic kernel is adapted
from [D3's ISC-licensed implementation](https://github.com/d3/d3-geo-projection).
The equatorial aspect intentionally differs from D3's default Peirce rotation.
