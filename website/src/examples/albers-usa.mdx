This focused example shows the experimental [`createAlbersUsaMap`,
`getMapLayout`, `fitMapLayout`, and `pickMapLayout`
APIs](/docs/modules/geospatial/discontinuous/). States are clipped at region
boundaries, and the cursor readout resolves geographic coordinates through the
frontmost inset. The example enables the five inhabited U.S. territory insets
with `{includeTerritories: true}` alongside Alaska and Hawaii. Alaska is
displayed at 35% linear scale. The preset's broad geographic masks are not
political borders or exact D3 inset clips. Minor outlying islands are outside
this example's coverage.

State boundaries come from [Natural Earth 1:10m](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/), public domain.
See the [standalone source](https://github.com/visgl/math.gl/tree/master/examples/albers-usa) and the [discontinuous projection guide](/docs/modules/geospatial/projection/developer-guide/discontinuous-projections).
