@math.gl/earth

The earth group collects Earth model, solar, and timezone utilities. Specific utilities are available from their subpaths.

Feature support​

Earth-model, observational-sky, and civil-time capabilities, including the limits of optional physical models.

Support describes the public API in the current repository. Notes identify limits and experimental features.

Featuremath.gl supportNotes
Geoid height grids and interpolation✅ ImplementedGeoid APIs parse/query optional GeographicLib-style grid data.
Sun, Moon and planetary sky state✅ ImplementedSun/sky APIs provide observer coordinates, lighting approximations and celestial appearance helpers.
Bright-star catalogs and rendering data✅ ImplementedOptional catalog data, proper-motion helpers and star-layer records keep data and rendering integration explicit.
Timezone lookup and civil-calendar arithmetic✅ ImplementedTimezone APIs combine approximate geography with runtime timezone rules and explicit local-time disambiguation.
Explicit UT1/TT sky contexts✅ ImplementedReusable sky contexts and snapshots accept explicit time-scale inputs within their documented ephemeris/model scope.
Globally calibrated visibility/lighting tiers◐ PartialShared sky models are implemented; observational calibration, star visibility integration and tier budgets remain in the sun roadmap.
Gravity/magnetic spherical-harmonic models❌ Not implementedNo general coefficient-backed gravity or geomagnetic field engine; optional Earth models are G11 scope/design work.
Qualified ICRF/ITRF/TEME state transformations❌ Not implementedFull versioned Earth-orientation/time providers and state-vector frame derivatives remain celestial-frame roadmap work.

Usage​

import {Geoid} from '@math.gl/earth';
import {getSunDirection} from '@math.gl/earth/sun';
import {lookupTimezoneAsync} from '@math.gl/earth/timezone';

The group root exports geoid, sun, and timezone namespaces. The existing @math.gl/earth, @math.gl/earth/sun, and @math.gl/earth/timezone package imports remain available.
