# Community

## Open governance and community

math.gl is part of Open Visualization, an OpenJS Foundation collaboration space for open source visualization projects. The vis.gl Technical Steering Committee oversees math.gl. [Open Visualization](https://www.openvisualization.org/) · [vis.gl governance](https://github.com/visgl/tsc)

You can contribute by reporting a bug, improving a guide or example, reviewing a change, or submitting code and tests.

## Meet the community

The annual Open Visualization Collaborator Summit brings contributors together to share their work and discuss what comes next. Between summits, meet other contributors on OpenJS Slack or join an Open Visualization community meeting. [Summits](https://www.openvisualization.org/summits) · [OpenJS Slack](https://slack-invite.openjsf.org/) · [Meeting calendar](https://www.openvisualization.org/#get-involved)
